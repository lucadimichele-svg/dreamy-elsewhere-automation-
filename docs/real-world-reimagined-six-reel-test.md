### Specific production rules for experiment
- **Keep the actual real landmark dominant and recognizable in every one of the five source images.** It is NOT enough to show a generic snowy New York skyline, a fantasy Italian canal with an anonymous arch, a generic suspension bridge, or an anonymous Greek village; the named famous place must be legible by its characteristic architecture/geography.
- Only the *world transformation* changes: ice age, overgrown, midnight fantasy, apocalypse, future, etc. Five transformations must be clearly visually different from each other and original 9:16 individual images, never crops of one collage.
- For each scheduled slot, **read and use the pre-saved plan**. If visual generation struggles, repair a sub-variant, reduce unnecessary details or change the weather/era while preserving the **same famous landmark and its five-image umbrella question**. Do not fall back to generic "extraordinary world" topics during this particular test unless the landmark is truly impossible to create after independent creative recovery, in which case report inability honestly rather than passing unrelated images off as the named landmark. Keep core subject real.
- If a sub-variant produces the real recognizable place but not the intended weather/era/material, re-label that sub-variant honestly and update its plan BEFORE the audit/manifest. Background detail is not an approval blocker. Visual identity of the place and image source technical validity are the only important checks; no subjective aesthetics/beauty scoring.
- Keep standard production, per-image SHA/visual audit and Drive manifest process, existing licensed music, typography, 15s/5 frames, and all four regular daily publish slots **unchanged**; no extra or make-up Reels, no manual approvals.
- The current day's 2026-11-22 S4 is NOT part of the experiment and must not be changed.
- Mark results by exact KEY. Compare completed 24h and 72h outcomes to earlier fantasy-only Reels: views, average viewing time, view duration / reel duration, likes, comments, shares, saves and follows (only when accessible). A small six-reel exploratory test cannot conclusively identify causality because timing, content and baseline can change.
